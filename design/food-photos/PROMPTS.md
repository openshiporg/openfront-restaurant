# Food photos: art direction and prompts

For the 22 seeded menu images in `public/images/`. Every store that uses the seed links these files by `imagePath`, so they are replaced in place under the same name and extension. Drop the finished renders into `design/food-photos/generated/` using the exact filename listed against each prompt (any image format; they are converted to match the extension on install).

## The set: "on the pass"

The storefront is built on the pass, the steel shelf between the kitchen and the counter where a finished order is set down under the heat lamps and waits for its ticket to be called. Every photo is that moment: the dish has just landed and it is about to be picked up. Shoot all 22 as one set, as if they were taken in one session on the same shelf with the same lamp.

- **Surface.** A brushed stainless steel counter with fine horizontal grain and soft, low-contrast reflections. It is clean, with no rivets, seams or edges in frame. In the lit centre the steel reads as a light, cool blue-grey (about `#C9CED3`) and falls off gently toward the edges. No cutting boards, slate, marble, wood or linen.
- **Background.** The same steel, rising out of focus into a slightly darker steel backsplash (about `#9DA4AB`). Behind the dish there is only out-of-focus steel. No kitchen clutter, no people and no hands.
- **Light.** One warm heat lamp above and slightly behind the dish (2700K, amber) gives a warm rim on the top edges and a glow on the bun, crust and glaze. A soft cool fill from the front (5600K, low) keeps the shadows readable. This makes a gentle warm pool on the steel under the food, with the frame edges cooler and slightly darker. The highlights are soft and unclipped, and there is no hard flash or ring light.
- **Angle.** Every shot is a three-quarter view from the front, the camera 30 to 35 degrees above the counter at a 50mm-equivalent focal length. The dish sits dead centre, filling about 55 to 60 percent of the frame width, with even steel margins on all sides so the image can be cropped to 1:1, 4:5, 4:3 or 16:10 without cutting the food. The focus plane is on the front of the dish, at roughly f/4, so the back softens slightly.
- **Styling.** This is honest counter food served the way this kitchen hands it over. Burgers and sandwiches sit on a square of plain white, unprinted deli paper, slightly creased. Fries and sides come in plain white paper boats or a small steel cup. Drinks are in clear glass or a plain white paper cup with a plain lid. Desserts are on a small plain white enamel plate. Each prompt describes one portion, built exactly as its menu description says, glossy where it should be, with crumbs and drips allowed. Nothing is overdressed: no scattered herbs, no garnish the menu doesn't list, and no sauce swooshes.
- **Colour grade.** Natural and appetising, with warm food against cool steel. Browns and golds carry a little extra warmth, greens stay fresh and not neon, and the steel stays neutral to cool. Contrast is moderate, without crushed blacks or HDR halos, with very light film grain. All 22 match each other.
- **Never.** No text, letters, logos, brand marks, stickers or printed tickets. No cups with logos, no props such as cutlery, napkins or condiment bottles unless the prompt names them, no hands, no smoke or steam effects, no bokeh balls, no vignette darker than the steel falloff, no white seamless background and no transparent background.
- **Output.** Square 2048 × 2048, photographic, opaque. One dish per image.

### Shared prompt prefix

Paste this before every item prompt:

> Professional food photograph, one dish set down on a clean brushed stainless steel restaurant pass with fine horizontal grain, just placed under a warm amber heat lamp above and slightly behind it, soft cool front fill, warm pool of light on the steel under the food, out-of-focus darker steel backsplash behind, three-quarter front view from 30 degrees above, 50mm, f/4, dish centred filling 55 percent of the frame with even steel margins, natural appetising colour, warm food against cool neutral steel, subtle film grain, square, no text, no logos, no hands, no props.

## Install

When the renders land in `design/food-photos/generated/`, each one is cropped square on centre, resized to 1200 × 1200, and written over `public/images/<same filename>`, with JPEG for `.jpg` and PNG for `.png`. The filenames, extensions and database rows all stay the same.
